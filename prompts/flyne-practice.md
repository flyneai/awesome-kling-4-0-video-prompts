# Flyne AI practice prompts / 原创练习

[Home](../README.md) · [X cases / 视频来源](../docs/X-VIDEOS.md) · [Model selection / 模型选择](../docs/FLYNE.md)

Two original, untested exercises inspired by the workflow lessons in the linked posts. They are not the authors' prompts and did not produce the linked videos. Timings are creative targets, not verified Flyne settings. Use an available mode; the Flyne 4.0 preview currently selects Kling 3.0 Turbo.

以下两条为另写的未实测练习，不是作者原提示词，也不是来源视频的生成记录。时长是创作安排；请按账号可用模式调整。

## 1. One question, one answer / 一问一答

[Source discussion: Riccardo Wolf](https://x.com/WolfRiccardo/status/2104613767212359878). Prepare an optional authorized two-person reference. Target: 10 seconds, 16:9. Use native dialogue only if the selected mode supports it.

```text
One continuous ten-second medium two-shot in a quiet bookshop. Two fictional adults stand on opposite sides of a wooden counter. Maya, in a green cardigan, remains on the left. Daniel, in a navy jacket, remains on the right, holding one closed red book. Preserve their faces, clothes, positions and the book throughout. Fixed camera at eye level, soft afternoon light.
0–2s: Maya looks at the book; Daniel notices her glance.
2–5s: Maya says in relaxed British English, "Did you find the ending?" Only Maya's mouth moves during this line.
5–8s: Daniel pauses, smiles slightly and replies, "I think it found me." Only Daniel speaks. Keep the book in his hands.
8–10s: They share a quiet smile. Hold the same composition.
Sound: low room ambience, clear alternating speech, no music. No overlapping voices, subtitles, added people, camera cuts, exchanged clothes or duplicated books.
```

**Review / 检查：** speaker assignment, mouth movement, eyelines and book ownership. If speech fails, test the silent action first, then add one line. 检查说话人与口型、视线和书的归属；失败时先测试无对白动作，再加一句对白。

## 2. A singer follows one musical phrase / 一句旋律的表演

[Source workflow: Simon Mayr](https://x.com/simonmeyer_/status/2104635206439198880). The creator discloses sponsorship. Prepare an authorized adult character still and an original or licensed eight-second vocal track. Requires a mode that explicitly supports audio/video references; if absent, generate a silent visual and add music in editing without claiming verified lip sync.

```text
Use the character still only for the adult singer's appearance and clothing. Use the supplied eight-second vocal track for vocal timing only. Do not copy any visual content from the audio-carrying file. One continuous medium close-up in a small rehearsal room, warm side light, plain background.
0–2s: The singer takes a natural breath and looks slightly above the lens.
2–6s: The singer performs the supplied phrase. Align mouth movements to the actual syllables and pauses. Keep head movement small and both hands below frame. The camera moves slowly forward, with no cuts.
6–8s: The singer finishes the phrase and holds a relaxed expression while the camera stops.
Preserve face, hair, clothing and background. Use the supplied track without additional lyrics or voices. No microphone appearing from nowhere, subtitles, face changes, scene changes or sudden zooms.
```

**Review / 检查：** compare mouth openings and phrase endings against the track; keep the output and actual model in a [test record](../docs/TEST-RECORD.md). 逐段核对口型与收尾，记录真实模型；来源成片的长度不能当作模型单次生成时长。
